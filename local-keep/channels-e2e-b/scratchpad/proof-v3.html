What changed in the tests, and why

  • testA device is now cut off only after its page has stopped fetching code. The tests used to lose the connection one to three seconds after a page opened. The app is then still fetching the code of the views it expects next; the failed fetches make it say "New version available", and with the flag on a held channel might not open, typing went to the command palette, and an offline write came back as "Failed to send message". Nearly every intermittent failure on this head had those failed fetches in its trace. That moment is real, and happens with and without the flag, so it has a test of its own (REC-04) instead of being mixed into every offline test.
  • defect, both buildsREC-04: a page cut off just after it opened says a new version is available. Failed 5 of 5 with the flag on and 5 of 5 with it off. It is on main too. With the flag on the same moment does more harm, because more depends on working offline.
  • testWRT-13 now has the other person write only after the laptop's message is at the services. I did not see the order differ in my three failure traces (what failed was the phone's writes not arriving), but the order was left to chance.
  • limitThe wait has a cost. While waiting, a connected device left alone also brings its recent channels up to date, so OFF-09, OFF-10 and SYN-05 cannot tell "held" from "fetched in that pause". With the old step on this head, OFF-09 and SYN-05 still held 5 of 5; OFF-10 held 3 of 5, and its failures had the failed code fetches.

Where I disagree with B's owner

  • WRT-04 is not fixed. Reported as 0 failures in 11; here it failed 10 of 15, and one timed delivery took 301 s after a reload.
  • MSG-13 is not gone. Reported as 0 in 14; here 3 of 5, flag on only.
  • The early-offline hazard is real, but it does not "blank or reload the page". What the traces show is the new-version notice, a held channel that says it cannot load, typing landing in the command palette, and an offline write returned as failed once online. The test change is agreed; the hazard is kept as REC-04.
  • WRT-13's order at the services was not what failed in my traces. The test is made deterministic anyway.
  • OFF-04 is said to be unfixed. It held 5 of 5 in the whole-suite runs and 2 of 5 with the old step; it stays marked.

What is not proven

  • No channel regressions: not shown. MSG-13 and CMP-13 fail in some flag-on runs and never with the flag off, and the flag-off suite ran once on this head.
  • Five runs, not a quiet machine. A failure rarer than about one in five can be missed.
  • The third column is not a like-for-like comparison with the first two: the offline step changed. The fourth column is the like-for-like one, from the device tests alone.
  • Not tested end to end: how long the first send after opening takes (the owner says 1 to 2 s), jumping to an older held message offline, a sequence that goes down, the prewarm limits, thirty-day eviction, a storage reset with queued writes, document discussions, phones and the desktop app. OFF-07 still accepts "there are more" without a count.
