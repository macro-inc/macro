Channels kept on the device: what end-to-end tests prove

Approach B, flag on, branch synoet/channels-local-sync-pr5-writes · 15 November 2026 · pull request #10868 (ready for review, not merged)

This is not proof that B works. The tests show that the core of B holds every time (catching up, writing offline, sending a lost write once, prewarming) and that B has two defects in every run, one regression of the existing suite in every run, and a dozen behaviours that fail in some runs: channels that do not open offline, an out-of-date copy shown offline, and messages lost or reordered. Rates below come from a machine at load 50, shared with another session's test suite; quiet-machine rates are given where they were measured and differ.

What holds every time

10 of 10Coming back to a channel, reconnecting, reopening the app or reopening after more than a thousand changes shows every edit, deletion, reply and reaction (SYN-01–04, 06, 09, 10). Losing access shows no kept messages (SYN-08). Sending, editing, deleting, reacting and replying offline arrive once, in order (WRT-02, 06, 08). A write whose answer was lost is in the channel once (WRT-05). A refused write shows "Failed to send message" and leaves the list (WRT-09). A held channel shows at once on a very slow link (OFF-03) and stays up when scrolled past what is held (OFF-05). An unread channel never opened opens offline after the app was left alone (PRE-01, 14 of 14).

Every new test, its ten runs and its break proof

Runs: how often the test's check held in ten runs. Proof: the deliberate break of the product under which the test failed. For a test whose check is marked as failing sometimes, the test itself cannot go red; its proof is that the marked check failed under the break.
