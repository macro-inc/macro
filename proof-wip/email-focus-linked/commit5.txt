fix(email-focus): recheck a thread and refill an inbox once who reads it changes

Why: the sweep admits an inbox with the linked users it remembered, and a
queued backfill pass kept the linked users of the look that queued it. When
a user was unlinked in between, a thread's read found the inbox out of the
rollout and was settled as checked at its latest mail, and the pass counted
the inbox as filled, so a re-link left those threads unclassified until new
mail arrived.

What:
- The sweep never settles a NotEnabled thread; it stops walking that inbox
  and counts it in SweepReport::inboxes_left.
- Each look gives every waiting backfill the linked users it just read.
- A pass that found its inbox out of the rollout, at the start or partway,
  is dropped rather than counted as filled, so the next look queues it again.
- The runbook says an inbox someone still enrolled reads keeps being
  classified after a user is removed.
