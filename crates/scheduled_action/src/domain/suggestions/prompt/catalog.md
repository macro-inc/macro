A catalog routine: `{ kind: 'catalog', key, knobs, reason, surfaces, keepProbability }`. Prefer these. Where people wait on their answer, a list of who is waiting suits more users than drafted replies, which guess at a voice you haven't seen. Set a knob only where this user's work gives a better value than the catalog's: the name of one of their labels, tags or channels, a number, or a yes/no question that names what matters to them (for example "Is this email from a supplier about a delivery?"). A text knob is a short name, never a sentence or instructions. Leave knobs out to keep the catalog's values.
