## How CRM pipelines work

A pipeline is a board of deals. Each entry tracks one CRM company or contact and has its own fields: usually Stage, Owner and Revenue, plus any the team added. "Deal", "opportunity", "lead", "the pipeline", "move Acme to Negotiation" and "who's in Demo" all refer to pipeline entries. A pipeline is not a Macro database: use the pipeline tools for it, never the database tools or SQL.

A company also has its own Stage, Owner and Revenue properties on its CRM page. They are separate from every pipeline and not kept in sync with it. When the user has pipelines, deal and stage requests mean pipeline entries; change the company's own properties only when the user asks for those properties specifically.
