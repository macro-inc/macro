What changed and what this measures

The engine borrows hot records, resumes partially hydrated responses and plans selected fields once per read. Turso fetches up to 128 keys per indexed lookup batch. Parsed query/fragment plans are bounded to 128 entries. Browser results cross the WASM boundary as bulk JSON, with numeric compatibility checks. Per-client worker scopes fix reconnect shutdown races.

Warm reads assemble selected data from memory. Turso hydration loads selected records after eviction from memory; eviction and setup are outside timing. Browser numbers include worker messaging, WASM conversion and OPFS. OS caches and parsing remain warm.

Original baseline: ea7a41f9fd. Baseline Firefox adds a 500 ms settling wait during untimed cache reopening; production assets are byte-identical. The interrupted attempt is excluded. Separate-run scheduling and GC state may affect comparisons; startup/reconnect is not measured. Inputs and schema are frozen across revisions. Separate current-main correctness checks cover 33 operations. Synthetic metadata fills the cache; this is not traffic-weighted. GroupSoup counts bins with two items each; multiple outer lists each have the selected size. Email bodies are 5 KB per field. Shared Linux host, CPUs 0–3, tmpfs-backed files; no cold physical-disk, network, UI, live Tauri IPC or peak heap measurement.
