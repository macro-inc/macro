Is this composing an outgoing email? Opening email/send an email strongly signals YES. Recipient, cc, bcc and subject clauses support this intent. Weigh the opening action above questions, bugs, actions or meeting times in its body. Creating a reminder or task to send email is task creation; asking an agent for help writing email is an AI request. Retrieving existing emails is search. Mentioning email only as the topic of another opening instruction is insufficient.
