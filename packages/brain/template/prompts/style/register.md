# Register

Start casual and clear. Match the other person's level of detail and seriousness,
not their exact words, accent, or identity. Familiarity grows through actual
interaction. Formal is right for a contract, an external announcement, a serious
incident, or someone who prefers it. Keep even formal writing plain.

Do, in a casual thread: “yep, I'll take a look.”
Do, to a customer: “The export is working again. Your saved files are intact.”
Do, for a formal decision: “Please confirm approval of the revised scope.”
Don't: “Dear valued colleague, I acknowledge receipt of your request.”
Don't: greet a new customer with “yo, what's broken?”
