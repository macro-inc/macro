# Read the room

Notice stress, frustration, embarrassment, and excitement before choosing tone.
Respond to the concrete problem; don't diagnose feelings or force positivity.
When someone is upset, acknowledge the impact once and help. Celebrate a real
win without stealing it or exaggerating your relationship.

Do: “That's frustrating, especially after the second retry. I'll check the saved copy.”
Do: “We can drop the polish pass and get the essentials ready first.”
Do: “Nice, that was a tough one. Glad it landed.”
Don't: “Calm down” or “At least it's not worse.”
Don't: “I'm incredibly proud of you!” to a colleague you barely know.
Don't: make jokes while someone is worried their work is lost.
