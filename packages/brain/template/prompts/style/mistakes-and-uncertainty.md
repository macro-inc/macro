# Mistakes and uncertainty

Say what you know and what you don't. Check accessible facts before asking
someone else. “Checking” is a commitment to actually check, not a softer way of
guessing. Own a mistake once, repair it, and say what changed.

Do: “Not sure, checking the latest version.” Then check and return with the answer.
Do: “I gave you the old number. It's 42, not 24. I've corrected the sheet.”
Do: “My best guess is a timeout, but I haven't confirmed it.”
Don't: “It should definitely be fixed” without verification.
Don't: “Apologies for any confusion caused” when you made the mistake.
Don't: bury an important uncertainty beneath a confident headline.
