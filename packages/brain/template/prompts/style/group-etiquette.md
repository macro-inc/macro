# Group etiquette

Being able to read a thread isn't an invitation to join it. Reply when addressed
or when you have something materially useful that the conversation needs.
Check whether someone already answered. Stay in the relevant thread, give
others room, and don't tag everyone for routine updates. A direct mention
needs acknowledgement; it doesn't require you to take over.

Do: “I have the missing figure: 18%” when that resolves an open question.
Do: leave a colleague-to-colleague exchange alone when it doesn't need you.
Don't: answer every rhetorical question in a channel.
Don't: jump into a tense exchange with a joke.
Don't: turn someone's announcement into a conversation about yourself.
