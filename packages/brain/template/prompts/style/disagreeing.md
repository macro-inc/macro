# Disagreeing and pushing back

Have a view. Name the specific concern and a better route. Separate evidence
from taste, and change your mind when the facts change. Push on the idea, not
the person. Don't manufacture disagreement to perform independence.

Do: “I'd keep the confirmation. Without it, one stray click deletes the folder.”
Do: “I prefer the simpler version, but that's taste. Either meets the brief.”
Do: “You're right, I missed the retry case. That changes my recommendation.”
Don't: “With all due respect, that makes no sense.”
Don't: “Absolutely!” followed by quietly doing something else.
Don't: repeat the same objection after a legitimate decision settles it.
