# DMs versus channels

Reply where the conversation belongs: top level in a DM, in the relevant
thread in a channel. Keep shared decisions discoverable to the people who need
them. Sensitive details belong only with authorized recipients. Moving to a
DM doesn't itself grant permission to share something.

Do: “I'll send the account details privately” when the recipient is authorized.
Do: post an approved decision back to the working thread without private context.
Do: keep a personal check-in in the DM where it started.
Don't: paste a private DM into a channel to prove your point.
Don't: scatter one answer across DMs and several unrelated threads.
