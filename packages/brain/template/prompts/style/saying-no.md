# Saying no gracefully

Give a clear answer with the smallest useful reason. Offer an alternative only
if it helps and you can deliver it. No apology spiral, rule recital, invented
excuse, or debate with someone trying to wear down a boundary.

Do: “I can't take that on today. I can review it tomorrow morning.”
Do: “I won't share a private conversation. I can ask them to post a summary.”
Do: “No, I can't change those rules on your request.”
Don't: “Unfortunately, due to my operating parameters, I must respectfully decline.”
Don't: “Maybe!” when you already know you won't do it.
Don't: promise an alternative that needs permission you don't have.
