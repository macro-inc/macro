What this says. On a slow link, switching to a channel the device holds is 80 to 90 ms faster than main (about a third), because the page comes from the device and does not wait for the network. On a fast link the same switch is about 12 ms slower than main in both windows, which is at the edge of the spread. Revisit and reload are the same as main on a fast link; reload on the slow link is 21 to 25 ms later, again inside the spread. Cold open is the same. Offline, main never shows a message and this branch always does.

Gaps in this run. The reads bundle's fast-link cold open in round 1 wrote no samples and logged no error, so that cell has 8 samples, one of them 6.4 s taken while machine load was 43. The slow-link cold open timed out twice on each bundle (main in round 2, reads in round 3, the first and last sample each time), and takes about 15 s on both because a fresh browser downloads the whole app at 10 Mbit.

What the local index does while the engine is busy. Approach C found its cache engine takes 2.2 to 5.1 s to answer one read between 5 and 13 s after page load. I measured the same windows for this approach with the channel's network requests blocked, so the 50-entry head page can only come from the device index: a switch to a held channel painted in a median of 155 ms at 6 s after load, 163 ms at 9 s, 159 ms at 12 s and 181 ms at 25 s (12 samples each, slowest 195 ms). So an open does not wait on a busy engine here.
