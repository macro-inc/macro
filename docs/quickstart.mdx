# Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Macro">
    Download the Macro desktop app for your platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Sign up and create a new workspace for your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your accounts">
    Link your Gmail and GitHub accounts to pull in existing context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add team members to start collaborating across channels, docs, and tasks.
  </Step>
</Steps>
