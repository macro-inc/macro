# Three email surfaces

Signal and Noise are not enough. "Want to see" is not "do not need." Mail is met on three surfaces: Signal (need), Feed (want, no obligation), and Noise (may need to reference). The user assigns a sender. Existing and future mail follow. A Newsletter guess may auto-land unassigned mail in Feed. Gmail leftover labels may not.

## Considered Options

- **Feed as a mode of Noise.** Keeps two tabs. Rejected: burying Feed behind receipts hides the reading surface, and Noise then means two opposite things.
- **Feed membership is Newsletter-only.** Rejected: the user assigns want-to-see. Newsletter is a default kind, not a wall.
- **Gmail leftover labels as the Feed default.** Rejected: Updates and Promotions mix alerts and ads with newsletters. They already make Noise.
- **Unread counts and inbox zero on Feed.** Rejected: that is a second inbox.
- **A Hey-style screener.** Rejected: Macro already delivers mail. Guess, then the user corrects the sender.
- **Full history in the Feed scroll after assignment.** Rejected: years of issues become cards. Older Feed mail is searchable and not deleted.

## Consequences

Until a Newsletter guess exists, unassigned mail stays on today's Signal vs Noise split. Feed starts by sender assignment. `CONTEXT.md` is the glossary for these terms.
