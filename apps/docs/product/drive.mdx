Drive contains the documents and files in your workspace: docs, databases, spreadsheets, canvases, PDFs, images, videos, code files, and uploads.

Attachments shared in channels are imported to Drive. Email attachments are extracted to Drive, but they stay out of the list until you filter for them. Choose **Filter → Files → Email attachments**. **Default**, which Drive uses until you change it, hides them. Everything in Drive is searchable from [unified search](/product/search).

## Tabs

- **My Files**: files you own
- **Recent**: files you opened or edited, most recent first
- **Shared with me**: files shared with you

**Search Drive** searches the current tab or folder. **Filter** filters by type, [tag](/product/tagging), or creator. **Files → Default** hides email attachments. Choose **Email attachments** to list them. **Sort** sorts by modified, created, or viewed date.

## Folders

The Drive sidebar shows the folder tree. Click a folder to open it. Click the arrow next to a folder to expand it.

The folder creation dialog accepts a name, tags, and files or folders to upload.

## Creating and uploading

Click **New** in the Drive sidebar to create a document, database, spreadsheet, canvas, folder, or other file. Inside a folder, the menu reads **Create in** followed by the folder name, and new files are created in that folder. **Database** stays on the **New** menu for **My Files**, **Recent**, **Shared with me**, and the folders overview. A database is not placed in a folder.

Drag files from your computer onto a folder to upload them.
