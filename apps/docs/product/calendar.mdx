Calendar is a Macro block that brings your Google and Microsoft calendars into one view. Events are entities in your workspace, so you can search them, @mention them, and hand them to the [Macro agent](/product/agents) the same way you would a doc or a task. Edits you make in Macro sync back to the connected calendar.

Open it from **Calendar** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>g</kbd> \+ <kbd>r</kbd>.

### Connecting your calendar

Open **Settings → Calendar** to connect your Google or Microsoft accounts. Connect more than one account to see work, personal, and shared calendars together.

Choose which calendars appear in your view from the calendar settings.

### Find time with your team

Ask the Macro agent in a channel to find a time with your teammates. It checks shared availability across the selected people and returns times when everyone is free.

Under **Team sharing** in Calendar settings, use **Share with teammates** to choose **Busy blocks**, **Event details**, or **Nothing**. Private events stay as busy blocks. Under **Calendars that count as busy**, choose which calendars should block your time when teammates or agents check availability.

### Creating and editing events

Click an empty slot on the grid to start an event there, or drag across a range to set its length as you create it.

The event highlight takes the color of the calendar you're saving to. Switch calendars under the selections and the event color will switch to match. This way it's clear which calendar a new event is being added to.

You edit details inline on the event itself. Type the title and description directly, and click any other pill to open a small editor in place:

Once an event exists, you can also drag it to a new time and drag its edges to change its length, straight from the grid. Both sync back to the connected calendar.

### Copy availability

Click **Copy availability** in the topbar to turn your open time into pasteable text, labeled with your timezone and ready to drop into a reply.

Set your working hours from the settings control next to the button. By default they run 9:00 to 18:00 and skip weekends.

### Calendar and MCP

The [Macro agent](/product/agents) can read and change your calendar. Ask it to find a slot, book a meeting, move something, or RSVP, and it shows you a preview of the event before anything is written.

You can use the same tools over Macro's MCP server:

- `ListCalendars`
- `ListCalendarEvents`
- `GetTeamAvailability`
- `CreateCalendarEvent`
- `UpdateCalendarEvent`
- `DeleteCalendarEvent`

To connect it, go to **Settings → MCP server**.

### Turning calendar off

You can disconnect a calendar account from **Settings → Calendar**.

Macro deletes its copy of that account's events and gives up calendar access. Your calendar stays with Google or Microsoft and the inbox keeps syncing mail as normal. To turn calendar back on, grant access again.
