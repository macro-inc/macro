Home is where you check everything in your workspace. It is not only notifications.

A document you recently edited shows up here, even when nobody mentioned you. Email you sent, tasks you updated, and agent chats you started show up the same way. Home also includes email, messages, @mentions, tasks, and agent activity that need a look.

> To go to Home use shortcut <kbd>g</kbd> \+ <kbd>h</kbd>. <kbd>g</kbd> \+ <kbd>i</kbd> also works.

## Navigating

Use <kbd>j</kbd> and <kbd>k</kbd> or the arrow keys to move between items. The selected item opens in a preview next to the feed.

Press <kbd>enter</kbd> to open an item. Press <kbd>shift</kbd>\+<kbd>enter</kbd> to open it in a new split.

Items are grouped by time: **Last few minutes**, **Last hour**, **This morning**, **This afternoon**, **This evening**, **Earlier today**, **Yesterday**, then by date.

## Filtering

Press <kbd>f</kbd> or click the filter button next to **Home** to open **Filter Home**.

- **Status**: **Unread**, **Read**, or **All**
- **Type**: Email, Channels, Chats, Agents, and other item types

**Reset filters** clears all filters. Filters persist across reloads.

## Agent composer

With no item selected, Home shows an agent composer under **What should we work on?**. Press <kbd>enter</kbd> to start an [agent](/product/agents) chat. Type `@` to reference an item or `/` to attach a [skill](/product/skills).

**New chat** clears the preview and shows the composer.
