Home is a single feed of activity across your workspace:

- **Emails** from all connected accounts
- **Messages** from channels, threads, and DMs
- **Agents** that finish running or need an answer
- @mentions in **document** comments
- **Tasks** assigned to you
- **Files** shared with you
- Emails you sent and agent chats you started

> To go to Home use shortcut <kbd>g</kbd> \+ <kbd>h</kbd>. <kbd>g</kbd> \+ <kbd>i</kbd> also works.

## Navigating

Use <kbd>j</kbd> and <kbd>k</kbd> or the arrow keys to move between items. The selected item opens in a preview next to the feed.

Press <kbd>enter</kbd> to open an item. Press <kbd>shift</kbd>\+<kbd>enter</kbd> to open it in a new [split](/concepts/blocks#splits).

Press <kbd>e</kbd> to mark an item done. Press <kbd>cmd</kbd>\+<kbd>z</kbd> to undo. Press <kbd>shift</kbd>\+<kbd>↓</kbd> to select multiple items.

Items are grouped by time: **Last few minutes**, **Last hour**, **This morning**, **This afternoon**, **This evening**, **Earlier today**, **Yesterday**, then by date.

## Filtering

Press <kbd>f</kbd> or click the filter button next to **Home** to open **Filter Home**.

- **Status**: **Unread**, **Read**, or **All**
- **Type**: Email, Channels, Chats, Agents, and other item types

**Reset filters** clears all filters. Filters persist across reloads.

## Agent composer

With no item selected, Home shows an agent composer under **What should we get done in Macro?**. Press <kbd>enter</kbd> to start an [agent](/product/agents) chat. Type `@` to reference an item or `/` to attach a [skill](/product/skills).

**New chat** clears the preview and shows the composer.

## Signal and Noise

Macro sorts items into Signal and Noise. Signal contains important notifications. Noise contains newsletters, promotions, and other low-priority email.

On desktop, Home shows Signal. Noise email is in [Email](/product/email#signal-vs-noise).

## Mobile

On mobile, Home is **Notifications** in the dock. Use the pills at the top to switch between **Signal** and **Noise**. Swipe left on an item to mark it done. Pull down to refresh.
