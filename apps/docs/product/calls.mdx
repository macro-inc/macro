Macro calls are Macro's own call system, inside your workspace.

> ### Important Call Sharing Information
>
> Calls in team and public channels are shared with your team by default. After the call, team members can open the recording, transcript, and AI summary from **Calls** and search. Calls in direct messages and private channels are private by default, and standalone calls started from a meeting link are not shared with the team. Only participants have access.

To change whether a channel call is shared, use **Call sharing** in the call controls during the call. The change applies once everyone else on the call approves it, whichever way it goes; one decline cancels it. After the call, only the person who started it can propose a change, from **Sharing** in the call's side panel, and the others approve there too.

After a call ends, it's auto-transcribed and an AI summary is generated. The recording and a readable transcript are always available at the bottom of the call.

From the calls tab, you can ask AI to help you work through your calls: searching transcripts, summarizing, and more.
