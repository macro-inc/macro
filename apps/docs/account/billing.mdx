Macro has two paid plans, priced per person. The price is the same whether you're solo or on a team.

|  | Free | Premium | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | \$0 | \$40 / month | \$200 / month |
| AI agent | Fast model only | All models | All models |
| AI usage included each month | Limited | \$40 | \$200 |
| Beyond included AI | — | Credits or usage billing | Credits or usage billing |
| Storage | 5 GB | 1 TB | 1 TB |

Teams require a paid seat for every member; there's no free plan for teams. Each seat has its own plan: a team can mix Premium and Max seats, and team admins choose the plan for every member from the **Plan** menu next to their name in **Settings → Team**. Moving a seat is prorated for the rest of the billing period. Your current plan is shown in **Settings → Billing**, with **Upgrade** buttons if you're on the free plan and an **Upgrade to Max** button on Premium (on a team, that moves only your own seat).

## AI usage

Every paid plan includes a monthly amount of AI usage worth the plan's price, measured at Macro's usage rates: Premium includes \$40 of AI each month and Max includes \$200. Each AI request draws on that amount based on the model and the tokens it used. On a team, seats pool together and each seat adds its own plan's amount, so a five-seat Premium team shares \$200 of AI each month, and a team with three Premium seats and one Max seat shares \$320.

**Settings → Billing** shows how much of this period's included AI you've used, when it resets, your credit balance, and your usage billing settings. Included usage resets at the start of each billing period; it doesn't roll over.

When the included amount is used up, AI requests pause until one of these applies:

- **Credits** — prepaid AI usage you buy in \$10, \$25, \$50, or \$100 packs. Credits are applied after your included amount, never expire, and carry over between periods.
- **Usage billing** — turn it on to bill AI beyond your included amount and credits to your card in \$10 increments, up to a monthly limit you choose. If a charge fails, usage billing pauses until you update your payment method and turn it back on.
- **Upgrading to Max** — five times the included usage for that seat. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the period. On a team, move the heavy users' seats to Max from **Settings → Team** and leave the rest on Premium.

On a team, the team owner buys credits and controls usage billing for everyone; members see the team's position but not the controls.

## Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are handled through Stripe. From **Settings → Billing**, choose **Manage** to open the billing portal, where you can update your payment method, download invoices, or cancel. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the billing period.

## Self-hosting

Macro is open source under the AGPLv3, and self-hosting is free, though some bundled services require your own licenses and it isn't our primary focus yet. See the [FAQ](/faq) for the current state of self-hosting, and contact [licensing@macro.com](mailto:licensing@macro.com) if you want to build on Macro under a different license.
