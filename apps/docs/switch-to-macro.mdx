Use the dedicated importers for high-volume migrations. For other tools, connect them through MCP and ask an agent to bring over the work you need. You can move one project first; you don't need to move everything at once.

## Import from Notion

Connect Notion, then ask the Macro Agent to import your workspace or a specific page and everything under it.

- Pages become documents, with folders preserving the page hierarchy.
- Databases become Macro databases, including their rows and relations.
- Task trackers become tasks.

Try: **“Import our Product space from Notion into Macro.”**

The importer prepares a plan for you to review before it creates anything. Choose whether the imported work is personal or shared with your team, then approve the plan. Content already imported and shared by a teammate is linked rather than duplicated.

## Import from Linear

Connect Linear, then ask the Macro Agent to import your workspace.

- Issues become tasks.
- Teams, projects, and initiatives become Macro projects.

Try: **“Import our Linear workspace into Macro.”**

Review the plan and sharing settings, then approve the import. Your Linear workspace stays unchanged. You can check progress or cancel an import; completed copies stay in Macro.

## Import from Slack

To bring over message history, use the Slack archive importer in **Settings → Team → Connections**. A team admin uploads a Slack export and selects the conversations to import. This option appears for teams with archive import enabled.

If you only need the channel structure, connect Slack and use **Import channels** to recreate channels with the members who are already on your Macro team. This imports channel names and membership, not messages.

### Keep Slack connected

You can keep Slack for older conversations or Slack Connect channels with external companies. In **Settings → Connectors**, add a Slack MCP server so agents can reference the conversations it exposes while you use Macro. The available history and actions depend on that server and its permissions.

## Use MCP for other tools

For ClickUp and other tools with an MCP server, connect the source to an agent that can also access Macro. Ask it to read the work you want to move and create the corresponding tasks, documents, or projects in Macro.

Try: **“Bring our open ClickUp tasks into Macro. Show me how you'll map their status, assignee, and project before you start.”**

What can be moved depends on the tools the source's MCP server exposes. Start with one project, check the result, then bring over the rest. You can also leave older work where it is and keep the connection so agents can reference it.

Use the Macro Agent with a connected source, or [connect your own agent to Macro through MCP](/AI/mcp/overview).

## Set up your workspace

[Open Macro](https://macro.com/app) or [get the iOS app](https://macro.com/download). Connect Gmail or Google Workspace to use email in Macro. Outlook and IMAP support are planned.

Create a team from the workspace menu at the bottom left and invite your teammates. Create a channel with **C**, then **M**. New team tasks and calls are shared by default; review [sharing and permissions](/product/sharing) before bringing in private work.

[Home](/product/home) brings together your messages, email, tasks, and agent activity. Press **Cmd-K** for the command menu, or **/** to search. Find the full keyboard shortcut list in the app.

To start with an agent, press **C**, then **A**, or mention **@Macro** in a channel. Connect the tools it needs and reference the work you want it to use. You can also [bring your own agent](/AI/bring-your-own).

## Check the import

Imports copy your work into Macro; they do not keep the original tool in continuous sync. Connected Gmail is different: it stays synced as an email account.

Start with one project. Check the imported content, assignees, and sharing before inviting the rest of the team or importing more. Keep the original workspace until you have checked what you need.

Need help with a larger migration? [Book a demo](https://macro.com/app/book/6de4003c-15e4-549f-b5a9-0e930bbf56e4/macro-demo) with the team. You can also [watch Desync's customer story](https://macro.com/posts/desync-customer-story).
