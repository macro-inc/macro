# Interface translation glossary

How each language words Macro's product terms, so translations made at
different times read as one product. Translate a new or changed message with
the words below, and add a row when a new product term appears. Each example is
an existing message; reuse its word for the term, not its whole phrase.

Across languages:

- Keep product and brand names in English: Macro, GitHub, Gmail, CRM, MCP, API.
- Keep `{{placeholders}}` exactly; move them to wherever the grammar needs.
- Use the language's own quotation marks (`„…“`, `« … »`, `「…」`) around
  quoted names.
- A trailing `…` means the item opens more choices; keep it.
- Match the sentence case of the English: only the first word and proper
  nouns are capitalized, unless the language capitalizes nouns (German).
- A tag (on documents and items) and a label (which groups channels in the
  channels rail) are different things. Where the language's word for label is
  already its word for tag, a label is a "group" instead: Spanish, Portuguese,
  Norwegian, Finnish, and both Chinese scripts.
- Prefer the shorter of two equally clear wordings: the settings sidebar is
  about 15 em wide, and menus cap at 22rem.
