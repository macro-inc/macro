feat(graphql-channel): replay committed channel changes

Why

Channel snapshots need a committed watermark and bounded catch-up reads so
the local cache can include writes that race with page loading.

What

Read the real sequence before timeline and thread pages. Map the domain
change feed's current messages, reply roots, activities, removed identities
and reset boundaries. Share activity projection across reads and verify
composed-schema parity, replay paging and watermark ordering.
